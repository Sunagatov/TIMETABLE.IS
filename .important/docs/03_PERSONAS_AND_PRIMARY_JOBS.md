# Personas and Primary Jobs

## Primary Persona — Owner Learner

The owner-user wants to:

- add words quickly
- group them by meaningful topics
- track what is weak vs strong
- see missing examples/part-of-speech gaps
- perform structured review sessions
- clean and enrich vocabulary with AI assistance
- restore mistakes from trash
- inspect progress with clear statistics

## Secondary Persona — Owner as Curator

The same person also acts as curator/maintainer of the vocabulary base and wants to:

- refine topic structure
- split overly broad topics
- import/export data safely
- use AI to accelerate categorization and enrichment
- keep the system internally consistent

## Primary Product Jobs To Be Done

1. "When I find a new word, I want to add it to the right topics with enough metadata so I can study it later."
2. "When my vocabulary set grows, I want Smart Review to surface the right words without manual picking."
3. "When topic structure becomes messy, I want audits and split planning to help me reorganize it."
4. "When I need to enrich many words, I want export/import workflows that work well with AI tools."
5. "When I accidentally delete something, I want reliable trash and restore behavior."
